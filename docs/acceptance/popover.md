# Popover

shadcn/ui's `popover` (Radix Popover) as Avalonia's `Flyout` on a `Button` (`Button.Flyout`, or any control's
`FlyoutBase.AttachedFlyout`) with Cosmo's `FlyoutPresenter` theme (`src/Aioros.Cosmo/Overlays/Flyout.axaml`):
shadcn's `PopoverContent` (`w-72 rounded-md border bg-popover p-4 text-popover-foreground shadow-md`, 4 px from its
trigger, Radix's `sideOffset`). `c:Popover.IsOpen` is the open state, two way. On the web Aioros writes the trigger as a
`<button popovertarget>` and the content as a `<div popover>` placed by CSS anchor positioning (the Popover API).

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| POPOVER-01 | Given the `popover-open` fixture on the desktop in each theme (an outline `Button` whose `Flyout` has `Placement="BottomEdgeAlignedLeft"` and `FlyoutPresenterClasses="w-80"`, an app style giving `FlyoutPresenter.w-80` a width of 320), when the button is clicked, then the presenter's border resolves shadcn/ui's `PopoverContent` background, text color, border color and width, 8 px radius (`OverlayCornerRadius`, `--radius-md`), 16 px padding and `shadow-md`, its box is the reference's within 0.5 px horizontally and 2.5 px vertically (4 px below the trigger, aligned with its left edge), and a `Flyout` without the class is 288 px wide (`w-72`). |
| POPOVER-02 | Given a `Button` with a `Flyout` holding a text box and a button on the desktop, when the focused button takes Return, then the flyout opens with the keyboard focus on the text box (Radix's first tabbable); Escape closes it and gives the focus back to the trigger; a click outside closes it; and a `Flyout` set without `VerticalOffset` sits 4 px from its target. |
| POPOVER-03 | Given a view whose model binds `c:Popover.IsOpen` two way on a `Button` with a `Flyout` (compiled bindings, no code-behind), whose content binds a text to the model and holds a button bound to a command with a parameter, when the model sets its open state, then the flyout opens and shows the model's text through its `ContentTemplate`; clicking the content's button runs the command with its parameter; and closing the flyout with Escape sets the model's open state back to false. |

| POPOVER-05 | Given the `popover-open` fixture and a combobox on the desktop, when the popover opens, then its surface (the presenter's root border) is shown at opacity 0, 95% and 8 px up, its origin at its top center, as tw-animate-css's `animate-in fade-in-0 zoom-in-95` with `slide-in-from-{side}-2` (opacity 0, 95% and 8 px towards the trigger, 150 ms with `ease`, `cubic-bezier(0.25,0.1,0.25,1)`), its transitions carry it to rest (opacity 1, no transform) once 150 ms have passed on its clock, and in a window with `c:Motion.IsReduced` it opens at rest, nothing moving; the combobox's popover enters the same way. |
