# Questionnaire

shadcn/ui's `questionnaire` from the radix base (`apps/v4/registry/bases/radix/ui/questionnaire.tsx`, on
`@shadcn/react`'s `Questionnaire` 0.3.1) in the radix-rhea style, on Cosmo's new-york-v4 tokens (decided with the user on
2026-10-19): one question at a time, its title, description and choices (18 px rounded cards of a 16 px indicator, a
label and a lettered shortcut), a freeform input, the progress `Question 1 of 3`, and Previous, Skip, Next and Submit;
a required question cannot be left unanswered, an optional one is answered or skipped. Cosmo's `c:Questionnaire` (an
`ItemsControl`: `CurrentIndex`, `Shortcuts`, `ShowProgress`, the actions' contents, `Submitted` and `SubmitCommand`),
`c:QuestionnaireItem` (`Title`, `Description`, `IsRequired`, `Status`, `IsInvalid`), `c:QuestionnaireChoice` (a
`RadioButton`: `Description`, `Shortcut`), `c:QuestionnaireCheck` (a `CheckBox`, for several answers) and
`c:QuestionnaireInput` (a `TextBox`).
