# Drawer

shadcn/ui's `drawer` (vaul, bottom) as `c:Drawer`: the dialog's surface with the `drawer` class (shadcn's `DrawerContent`:
the host's bottom edge, `rounded-t-lg border-t`, `max-h-[80vh] mt-24`, the 100 by 8 px handle `bg-muted` 16 px from its
top), its header centered (`p-4 gap-0.5 text-center`) and footer a column (`p-4 gap-2`). vaul's drag to dismiss is not
dragged down past a quarter of its height (or flicked) closes as vaul's, the mask fading as it goes, on the desktop and on
the web (framework need 103); Escape, a click on the mask and its close buttons close it too.
