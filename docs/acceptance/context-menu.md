# Context Menu

shadcn/ui's `context-menu` (Radix ContextMenu) as Avalonia's `ContextMenu` on any control, with Cosmo's `ContextMenu`
theme (`src/Aioros.Cosmo/Overlays/Menu.axaml`: shadcn's `ContextMenuContent`, the same surface as the dropdown menu's,
2 px right of the pointer) and the same `MenuItem` themes. Aioros does not write a `ContextMenu` on the web (framework
need 36).

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| CONTEXTMENU-01 | Given the `context-menu-open` fixture on the desktop in each theme (a 300 by 150 px area, the menu 208 px wide by an app style for `w-52`), when the area is right-clicked at its center, then the menu opens at the pointer (its box the reference's within 0.5 px horizontally and 2.5 px vertically: 2 px to the right of the pointer, its top at the pointer), its surface resolves shadcn/ui's `ContextMenuContent` background, border, radius, padding and `shadow-md`, and its items' boxes relative to it are the reference's within 0.5 px horizontally and 1 px vertically. |
| CONTEXTMENU-02 | Given an area with a context menu on the desktop, when it takes the keyboard focus and Shift+F10 (or the Apps key) is pressed, then the menu opens; Down moves to the next item, Return runs its command and closes the menu, and Escape closes it with the focus back on the area; and a right click on another place closes it. |
| CONTEXTMENU-03 | Given a view whose context menu items bind commands with parameters and a checkbox item two way to the model (compiled bindings), when an item is clicked on the desktop, then its command runs with its parameter and the checkbox item flips the model's value. |
| CONTEXTMENU-04 | Given the Context Menu page on the web, when it is served, then its area is written inside the framework's context span naming its menu, and the menu is a `<div popover role="menu">` holding its items (a command item a `role="menuitem"` submit button posting its parameter); a right click on the area in Chromium opens the menu without a request. |
| CONTEXTMENU-05 | Given the `context-menu-open` fixture on the desktop, when the menu opens at the pointer, then its surface is shown at opacity 0, 95% and 8 px to the left, its origin at its top start (Radix's context menu opens on its right side), as tw-animate-css's `animate-in fade-in-0 zoom-in-95` with `slide-in-from-{side}-2` (opacity 0, 95% and 8 px towards the trigger, 150 ms with `ease`, `cubic-bezier(0.25,0.1,0.25,1)`), and is at rest once 150 ms have passed, and in a window with `c:Motion.IsReduced` it opens at rest, nothing moving. |
| CONTEXTMENU-06 | Given the `context-menu-open` fixture on the web, when Chromium reads the menu's popover, then its `@starting-style` is `opacity: 0; transform: matrix(0.95, 0, 0, 0.95, -8, 0)`, its look while closed is `opacity: 0; transform: matrix(0.95, 0, 0, 0.95, 0, 0)` with its own 150 ms transitions (tw-animate-css's `animate-out fade-out-0 zoom-out-95`, which leaves without the slide; Cosmo's `:closing` look), its origin is `0% 0%`, and its computed transitions are opacity and transform over 0.15 s with `cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1)`, with `display` and `overlay` as `allow-discrete` (exits through the top layer) |
| CONTEXTMENU-07 | Given the `context-menu-open` fixture open on the desktop, when it closes, then a picture of its surface stands in the window's overlay layer where the surface stood, passing clicks through, with the surface's transitions (150 ms with `ease`) and its origin at its top start, and leaves for opacity 0 and 95% (tw-animate-css's `animate-out fade-out-0 zoom-out-95`), gone once 150 ms have passed; and in a window with `c:Motion.IsReduced` it closes at once, with no picture. Precedent: Radix's `Presence`, which keeps closed content only while its exit animation plays. |
