# Typography

shadcn/ui's typography page (utility classes, no component) as styles of Cosmo's `TextBlock` classes with `typography`
(`h1`, `h2`, `h3`, `h4`, `p`, `lead`, `large`, `small`, `muted`), `Border` classes for `blockquote` and `inline-code`,
and `c:List` (a sealed `ItemsControl` of bulleted `c:ListItem`s; `inside` for `list-inside`).

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| TYPOGRAPHY-01 | Given the `typography-headings` and `typography-text` fixtures in each theme on the web, when the pages are served, then `h1` and `h2` are written as `<h1>` and `<h2>`, and Chromium computes every heading's, paragraph's, quote's, list item's and code's font size, weight, line height, style and color as shadcn/ui's (the h1 in Geist Bold, the heaviest weight Cosmo ships, as Chromium resolves `font-extrabold` with the same files), the quote's 2 px `--border` left border and 24 px indent, the code's `--muted` box, 4 px radius and Geist Mono, and their boxes within 1 px except the h1's line breaks (`text-balance`), the quote italic, the headings' tracking (`-0.9px`, `-0.75px`, `-0.6px`), the headings written as `<h1>` to `<h4>` and the quote and code as `<blockquote>` and `<code>` (framework needs 40 and 41, resolved in Aioros `0.1.0-preview.408.cc55357`); the `p` and `ul` elements are not written yet. |
| TYPOGRAPHY-02 | Given the `typography-headings` and `typography-text` fixtures in each theme on the desktop, when they are shown, then every text has shadcn/ui's font size, weight, line height, letter spacing, style and color, the headings carry their heading level (1 to 4) for assistive technologies, the list's bullets are 5 px dots 17 px before their text 8 px apart, and every box is within 1.5 px of shadcn/ui's but the h1's lines (`text-balance`). |
| TYPOGRAPHY-03 | Given a view whose model binds a typography heading's text with `StringFormat`, a lead's visibility through a converter and a `c:List`'s `ItemsSource` with an `ItemTemplate` (compiled bindings, no code-behind), when the model adds an item through a command, then the heading's formatted count and the list follow, each new item with its bullet. |
| TYPOGRAPHY-04 | Given the `typography-text` fixture on the web, when the page is served, then the paragraph, the lead and the muted texts are `<p>`s, the small text a `<small>`, the list a `<ul>` of `<li>` items, the quote a `<blockquote>` and the inline code a `<code>`, as shadcn/ui's typography examples write them, and the fixture's pixels stay within FIDELITY's floor. |
| TYPOGRAPHY-05 | Given the texts shadcn/ui balances (`text-balance`) on the desktop: the `empty-demo` fixture's title and description, the `field-content` fixture's two-line description, the `item-header-footer` fixture's description and a tooltip's text, when they are laid out at a width that wraps them, then each keeps its line count and its lines' widths differ by no more than a word's width (the narrowest box that keeps the line count, as CSS's `text-wrap: balance`), its box keeping its width and its alignment; and a text without the balance is laid out as before. |
| TYPOGRAPHY-06 | Given the same texts on the web, when Chromium lays them out, then each is written with `text-wrap:balance` and wraps into the same number of lines as on the desktop. |
