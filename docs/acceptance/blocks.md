# Blocks

shadcn/ui's login and signup blocks (new-york-v4 `login-01` to `login-05`, `signup-01` to `signup-05`) composed from
Cosmo's components: a card or a bare form of `c:FieldGroup`s with labeled text boxes, a primary and outline buttons,
`c:FieldSeparator`, links, the Acme logo and, for `login-04` and `signup-04`, a placeholder image. Each block is a
Gallery page (`/blocks/{block}`) and a fidelity fixture (`/fidelity/{block}`), the reference rendering the block's page
at its content's height (`min-h-svh` released) in the 640 px fixture at a 1000 px viewport, so `md:` applies and `lg:`
does not. The app shell blocks (`dashboard-01`, `sidebar-01` to `sidebar-16`) are fixtures of shadcn/ui's pages in a
1280 by 800 viewport, the clock fixed on 2026-10-16, and Gallery pages in an 800 px high frame.
