# Tabs

shadcn/ui's `tabs` (Radix Tabs) as Avalonia's `TabControl` and `TabItem` with Cosmo's control themes
(`src/Aioros.Cosmo/Navigation/Navigation.axaml`): the tab strip `TabsList` (`bg-muted rounded-lg p-[3px] h-9`, as wide as
its tabs), each `TabItem` a `TabsTrigger` (`rounded-md border px-2 py-1 text-sm font-medium`, `--foreground` at 60% in
light, `--muted-foreground` in dark; selected: `--background`, `shadow-sm`, in dark `--input` border on `--input` at
30%), the selected tab's content 8 px below; `TabsKeyboard` gives Radix's keys.

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| TABS-01 | Given the `tabs-list` fixture in each theme on the web, when the page is served, then the tab control is a `role="tablist"` of `role="tab"` buttons with their `role="tabpanel"` panels (WAI-ARIA's tabs pattern, Aioros `0.1.0-preview.426.bcb6ce4`), each tab's 14 px medium text in shadcn/ui's color (the selected one `--foreground`, the others at 60% or `--muted-foreground`), and posting is not needed to switch: a click selects the next tab (`aria-selected="true"`) without a request and the arrow keys move between the tabs and select them, as Radix's automatic activation. |
| TABS-02 | Given the `tabs-list` and `tabs-demo` fixtures in each theme on the desktop, when they are shown, then the strip is a `--muted` box with 10 px corners and 3 px padding, 36 px high and as wide as its tabs at shadcn/ui's box within 2.5 px (Avalonia rounds each tab's text width up to a whole pixel), each tab 29 px high with 8 by 4 px padding, 8 px corners and shadcn/ui's text color, the selected tab painting shadcn/ui's background, border and `shadow-sm`, the disabled one at 50% opacity, a tab under the pointer turning `--foreground`, and the selected tab's content 8 px below the strip. |
| TABS-03 | Given a `TabControl` with four tabs, the last disabled, on the desktop, when the focused first tab takes Right, Left, End, Home and Left again, then the selection and the focus move to the second tab, back to the first, to the third (End skips the disabled tab), to the first and, looping, to the third, as Radix's roving focus with automatic activation. |
| TABS-04 | Given a view whose model binds a `TabControl`'s `ItemsSource` (each tab's header through `ItemTemplate`, its page through `ContentTemplate`) and `SelectedIndex` two way (compiled bindings, no code-behind), when the model selects the second tab and the user then selects the first, then the second tab's page shows its model's text, and the model's index follows the user back to 0. |
| TABS-05 | Given the Tabs page on the web, when a visitor selects the `Password` tab of the bound tabs and posts `Save password`, then the answer keeps the `Password` tab selected (`aria-selected="true"`, its panel shown) and reads the model's `Password saved` status. |
| TABS-06 | Given the `tabs-line`, `tabs-vertical` and `tabs-vertical-line` fixtures in each theme on the desktop (a `TabControl` with the `line` class, shadcn/ui's `variant="line"`, and with `TabStripPlacement="Left"`, its `orientation="vertical"`), when they are laid out, then the strip stands at shadcn/ui's box within 2.5 px and every tab has shadcn/ui's size within 1.5 px, the first at its place and each next one right after the last (Avalonia rounds each width up to a whole pixel): the line strip has no background or radius and its tabs stand 4 px apart with no background or shadow, the selected one marked by a 2 px `--foreground` line 5 px below it (on its right, 4 px out, when vertical), drawn outside the tab and the strip with nothing clipping it; a vertical strip is as wide as its widest tab, its tabs full width, left aligned and 30 px tall, and the content stands 8 px to its right. |
| TABS-07 | Given the `tabs-list` and `tabs-line` fixtures in each theme on the desktop, when a tab is hovered and when the selected tab takes the keyboard focus, then the hovered tab's text turns `--foreground` and the focused one has shadcn/ui's focus look: its 1 px `--ring` outline (drawn as a 1 px shadow, Avalonia having no outline) over the 3 px `--ring` at 50% ring, its border `--ring` (in the dark theme none for a line tab, shadcn/ui's `dark:…data-[state=active]:border-transparent`), and no `shadow-sm` for a line tab. |
| TABS-08 | Given the `tabs-line`, `tabs-vertical` and `tabs-vertical-line` fixtures in each theme on the web, when Chromium lays them out, then the strip, every tab, the selected tab's line and the content stand at shadcn/ui's boxes within 1 px with their colors, as TABS-06 states for the desktop. |
| TABS-09 | Given the `tabs-list` and `tabs-line` fixtures in each theme on the web, when Chromium computes a hovered tab and the selected tab with a visible keyboard focus, then their text color, border and focus ring (the outline as a 1 px shadow over the 3 px ring) are shadcn/ui's, as TABS-07 states for the desktop. |
| TABS-10 | Given a `TabControl` with `TabStripPlacement="Left"` and three tabs on the desktop, the first selected and focused, when Down, Down, Down, Up, Right and End are pressed, then the selection and the focus move to the second, the third, back to the first (looping), to the third, stay there on Right (a vertical strip ignores Left and Right, as Radix's `orientation="vertical"`) and stay on the third on End. |
| TABS-11 | Given the `tabs-vertical` fixture on the web, when the selected tab has the focus and Down then Up are pressed, then the second tab and then the first are selected and focused without a request, the list naming its `aria-orientation="vertical"`. |
