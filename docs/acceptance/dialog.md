# Dialog

shadcn/ui's `dialog` (Radix Dialog) as `c:Dialog` (`src/Aioros.Cosmo/Overlays/Dialog.cs`): a control that takes no room
where it is declared and, while its `IsOpen` (two way) is true, shows its `Content` on a `c:DialogSurface` (shadcn's
`DialogContent`: `max-w-lg rounded-lg border bg-background p-6 shadow-lg`, the close button at `top-4 right-4`) centered
on a 50% black mask over the nearest `c:OverlayHost` panel (or the window's overlay layer). `c:DialogHeader` is
`DialogHeader` with its title and description, `c:DialogFooter` `DialogFooter`, and `c:Dialog.Closes` `DialogClose`.
On the web, what a pending command opens in a host panel is written by Aioros as a modal `<dialog>` (ENGINE-143..146):
the Gallery's dialogs are opened by commands that await their answer.
