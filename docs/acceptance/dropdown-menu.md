# Dropdown Menu

shadcn/ui's `dropdown-menu` (Radix DropdownMenu) as Avalonia's `MenuFlyout` on a `Button` (`Button.Flyout`) with
Cosmo's `MenuFlyoutPresenter`, `MenuItem` and menu `Separator` themes (`src/Aioros.Cosmo/Overlays/Menu.axaml`):
shadcn's `DropdownMenuContent` (`min-w-[8rem] rounded-md border bg-popover p-1 shadow-md`, 4 px below its trigger), its
items (`px-2 py-1.5 rounded-sm text-sm`, highlighted `bg-accent`), checkbox and radio items (`ToggleType`, the check or
the dot at `left-2`, `pl-8`), labels (the `label` class), `inset` and `destructive` items, separators, shortcuts
(`InputGesture`, `text-xs tracking-widest text-muted-foreground`) and submenus (a `MenuItem`'s items, `shadow-lg`). On
the web Aioros writes the menu as a `<div popover>` of items, nested popovers for submenus.

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| DROPDOWN-01 | Given the `dropdown-menu-open` fixture on the desktop in each theme (a label, items with shortcuts, a submenu, separators and a disabled item, the presenter 224 px wide by an app style for `w-56`), when the trigger is clicked, then the presenter's border resolves shadcn/ui's `DropdownMenuContent` background, border, 8 px radius, 4 px padding and `shadow-md` with its box the reference's (4 px below the trigger, aligned with its left edge, within 0.5 px horizontally and 2.5 px vertically), every item, label and separator box relative to the presenter is the reference's within 0.5 px horizontally and 1 px vertically, each item's padding (8 by 6 px), 6 px radius, 14 px text, the label's medium weight, the disabled item's 0.5 opacity and each shortcut's 12 px muted text are shadcn's, a highlighted item takes `--accent` and `--accent-foreground`, and a submenu's chevron is a 16 px muted chevron. |
| DROPDOWN-02 | Given the `dropdown-menu-checkboxes` and `context-menu-open` items on the desktop, when the menu is open, then a checkbox or radio item has 32 px of left padding (`pl-8`), a checked checkbox item shows a 16 px check and a checked radio item an 8 px dot centered 15 px from its left edge, an unchecked one shows neither, an `inset` item or label has the same padding, a `destructive` item's text is `--destructive` and, highlighted, its background `--destructive` at 10% (20% in dark), and a submenu opens to the right with `shadow-lg`. |

Precedent: Radix's `Presence`, which keeps closed content only while its exit animation plays. |
