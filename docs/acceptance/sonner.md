# Sonner

shadcn/ui's `sonner` (its `Toaster`, sonner 2.0.7) as `c:Toaster` (`src/Aioros.Cosmo/Overlays/Toaster.cs`): the
`c:Toast`s added to the model's collection bound to its `Toasts` are shown by an Avalonia `WindowNotificationManager`
at a corner of the window (24 px in, bottom right by default) in a `c:ToastDeck`, sonner's stack (the newest in front,
two more peeking 14 px behind it, each 5% smaller; 14 px apart under the pointer or with `IsExpanded`), each card
sonner's toast as shadcn styles it (356 px, 16 px padding, `--popover`, `--border`, `--radius`, 13 px text, the kind's
icon, the action button). A toast stays 4 s (paused under the pointer); a loading toast stays until it is resolved
(sonner's `toast.promise`). A toast is swiped away toward its corner's sides as sonner's, and shows sonner's close button with
`CloseButton`. On the web Aioros writes the toasts a post showed as notices (ENGINE-147, SSR-155) in a deck the browser
keeps as sonner's stack, swiped the same way (framework need 103).
