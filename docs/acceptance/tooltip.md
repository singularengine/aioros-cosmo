# Tooltip

shadcn/ui's `tooltip` (Radix Tooltip) as Avalonia's `ToolTip.Tip` with Cosmo's `ToolTip` theme
(`src/Aioros.Cosmo/Overlays/ToolTip.axaml`): shadcn's `TooltipContent` (`rounded-md bg-foreground px-3 py-1.5 text-xs
text-background`) with its arrow, above its trigger. Cosmo sets shadcn's `TooltipProvider` defaults (no delay, Radix's
300 ms skip delay as Avalonia's `ToolTip.BetweenShowDelay`, `Placement="Top"`), shows the tip while its control has the
keyboard focus and hides it with Escape. On the web Aioros writes the tip after its control in a `role="tooltip"` span
shown by CSS on hover and focus.

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| TOOLTIP-01 | Given the `tooltip-open` fixture on the desktop in each theme, when the pointer rests on the outline button, then the tip opens at once and its bubble resolves shadcn/ui's `TooltipContent` background (`--foreground`), text color (`--background`), 12 px text on a 16 px line, 12 by 6 px padding and 8 px radius, its box is the reference's within 1 px, Avalonia's layout rounding (centered 10 px above the trigger, 28 px high), and its arrow is a 10 px square with a 2 px radius turned 45°, centered on the bubble 2 px above its bottom edge, in the bubble's color. |
| TOOLTIP-02 | Given controls with tips on the desktop, when one takes the keyboard focus with Tab, then its tip opens and Escape closes it, and blurring it closes it; a control with `ToolTip.ShowDelay="700"` hovered fresh shows nothing at once, while one hovered right after another tip closed opens at once (Radix's skip delay, `BetweenShowDelay` 300 ms by default); and a control's own `ToolTip.Placement` wins over Cosmo's `Top`. |
| TOOLTIP-03 | Given a view whose model provides a tip text through a binding with `StringFormat` and a `ToolTip` whose `Content` is bound to a model object drawn by a `ContentTemplate` (compiled bindings), when the tips open on the desktop, then they show the formatted text and the template's text, and changing the model updates the open tip. |
| TOOLTIP-04 | Given the Tooltip page on the web, when it is served and the trigger is hovered and then focused with the keyboard in Chromium, then the tip is a `role="tooltip"` element after its trigger, shown on hover and on focus with shadcn/ui's background, text color, 12 px text, 12 by 6 px padding and 8 px radius computed; it sits below its trigger without an arrow and the trigger has no `aria-describedby` (framework need 33). |
| TOOLTIP-05 | Given the `tooltip-open` fixture on the desktop, when the tip shows, then it (its bubble and arrow) is shown at opacity 0, 95% and 8 px down, its origin at its bottom center (`side="top"`, `slide-in-from-bottom-2`), as tw-animate-css's `animate-in fade-in-0 zoom-in-95` with `slide-in-from-{side}-2` (opacity 0, 95% and 8 px towards the trigger, 150 ms with `ease`, `cubic-bezier(0.25,0.1,0.25,1)`), and is at rest once 150 ms have passed, and in a window with `c:Motion.IsReduced` it opens at rest, nothing moving. On the web the tip keeps its own fade (framework need 77). |
| TOOLTIP-06 | Given the `tooltip-open` fixture showing its tip on the desktop, when the tip hides, then a picture of it stands where it stood, passing clicks through, with its transitions (150 ms with `ease`) and its origin at its bottom center, and leaves for opacity 0 and 95% (`data-[state=closed]:animate-out fade-out-0 zoom-out-95`, no slide), gone once 150 ms have passed; with `c:Motion.IsReduced` it hides at once. Precedent: Radix's `Presence`. |
| TOOLTIP-07 | Given the Tooltip page in Chromium, when the visitor rests the pointer on the trigger, then the tip enters as shadcn/ui's after its delay: an animation over 150 ms with `ease` from opacity 0, 95% and 8 px down (`fade-in-0 zoom-in-95 slide-in-from-bottom-2`) from its bottom center, and when the pointer leaves it transitions over 150 ms to opacity 0 and 95% (`fade-out-0 zoom-out-95`); for a visitor who asks for less motion neither plays. |
