# Sidebar

shadcn/ui's `sidebar` as `c:SidebarProvider` (a sealed `Panel` laying the sidebar beside the inset: `IsOpen` and
`IsMobileOpen` two way, `MobileBreakpoint` on the window's width, `ToggleGesture` Ctrl+B, `StateStore` and `StateKey`
for persistence), `c:Sidebar` (`Side`, `Variant` sidebar, floating or inset, `Collapsible` offcanvas, icon or none; the
header, content and footer rows; the mobile sheet), `c:SidebarHeader`, `c:SidebarContent`, `c:SidebarGroup`,
`c:SidebarGroupLabel`, `c:SidebarMenu`, `c:SidebarMenuItem` (`IsCollapsible`, `IsExpanded` two way),
`c:SidebarMenuButton` (`IsActive`, `Tooltip`, `sm`, `lg`, `outline`), `c:SidebarMenuSub`, `c:SidebarMenuSubButton`,
`c:SidebarMenuAction`, `c:SidebarMenuBadge`, `c:SidebarFooter`, `c:SidebarTrigger`, `c:SidebarRail`,
`c:SidebarSeparator` and `c:SidebarInset`.
