# Pagination

shadcn/ui's `pagination` as `c:Pagination` (a sealed `ItemsControl`, shadcn's `Pagination` and `PaginationContent`: its
items in a centered row 4 px apart, a navigation landmark named `pagination`), `c:PaginationPrevious` and
`c:PaginationNext` (sealed `Button`s: ghost, `px-3 gap-1`, Lucide's chevron beside "Previous" and "Next"),
`c:PaginationEllipsis`, and page buttons as Cosmo's buttons (`ghost icon`, the current one `outline icon`), as shadcn's
`PaginationLink` is `buttonVariants`.

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| PAGINATION-01 | Given the `pagination-demo` fixture in each theme on the web, when the page is served, then Previous, the pages 1 to 3 and Next are buttons 4 px apart with shadcn/ui's heights, vertical positions and colors, the pages as wide as shadcn/ui's, the current page outlined (`--background`, the outline's border) and the others ghost; Previous and Next show their chevrons and are as wide as shadcn/ui's (framework needs 49 and 50, resolved in Aioros `0.1.0-preview.408.cc55357`), the pagination is a `<nav aria-label="pagination">` landmark (need 41); the `<ul>`/`<li>` list, `aria-current="page"` and links are not written, since the pages are buttons that post their command. |
| PAGINATION-02 | Given the `pagination-demo` fixture in each theme on the desktop, when it is shown, then every button is at shadcn/ui's box within 1 px, Previous and Next draw their 16 px chevrons 4 px from their text in their text's color and turn `--accent` under the pointer, the current page has the outline look and the others the ghost look, the ellipsis is 36 px square, and the pagination is a navigation landmark named `pagination` whose Previous and Next are named `Go to previous page` and `Go to next page`. |
| PAGINATION-03 | Given a view whose model gives the pages (each an item with its number and whether it is current, shown through an `ItemTemplate` whose button binds `Classes.outline`, `Classes.ghost`, its command and its number as parameter) and binds Previous's and Next's commands (compiled bindings, no code-behind), when the user clicks Next and then the page 4, then the model's page is 3 then 4 and the outlined button is the model's current page. |
| PAGINATION-04 | Given the Pagination page on the web (the page 2 current), when a visitor posts the page 3 of the bound pagination, and on a fresh page posts Next, then each answer outlines the page 3 and reads `Page 3 of 10` (the current page is the model's, not a field the page posts back). |
| PAGINATION-05 | Given the `pagination-demo` fixture on the web in a viewport 390 px wide, when the page is served, then Previous and Next show only their chevrons, their labels hidden below 640 px as shadcn/ui's `hidden sm:block` hides them, keep their names ("Go to previous page", "Go to next page"), and no button's box overlaps the next one's. |
| PAGINATION-06 | Given the `pagination-demo` fixture on the web, when the page is served, then the navigation landmark holds a `<ul>` whose children are `<li>` items, each holding one of Previous, the pages, the ellipsis and Next, as shadcn/ui's `PaginationContent` and `PaginationItem`; the current page (2, outlined) alone carries `aria-current="page"`, as shadcn/ui's active `PaginationLink`; Previous and Next hold their chevron icons beside their labels; and on the Pagination page the bound pagination's current page carries it, page 3 after a visitor posts page 3. |
