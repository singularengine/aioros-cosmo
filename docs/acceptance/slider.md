# Slider

shadcn/ui's `slider` (Radix Slider) as Avalonia's `Slider` with Cosmo's control theme (`src/Aioros.Cosmo/Forms/Slider.axaml`):
a 6 px `--muted` track, its `--primary` range and a 16 px white thumb with a `--primary` border and `shadow-sm`, ringed on
hover and focus; `TickFrequency` with `IsSnapToTickEnabled` is Radix's `step`. A range with two thumbs is not ported
(Avalonia has no two-thumb slider).

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| SLIDER-01 | Given the `slider-states` fixture, when the page is served, then each `Slider` is an `<input type="range">` with a form field name, its `min`, `max`, the `step` of its tick frequency and its value, its `AutomationProperties.Name` as `aria-label`, and `disabled` when it is not enabled. |
| SLIDER-02 | Given `Slider`s in each theme on the desktop, horizontal at 50 and 33 (disabled) and vertical at 40, when they are shown, under the pointer and focused with the keyboard, then the track is 6 px of `--muted`, the range `--primary`, the thumb 16 px white with a 1 px `--primary` border and `shadow-sm` (with the 4 px `--ring` at 50% under the pointer and with the focus), each thumb's box relative to its slider is shadcn/ui's within 0.5 px, a vertical slider is at least 176 px high, and the disabled one has an opacity of 0.5. |
| SLIDER-03 | Given a `Slider` whose `Value` is bound two way, with a step of 1, on the desktop, when it is focused and Right, Up, Left, Home and End are pressed, and when the pointer presses its track at three quarters, then its value goes to 51, 52, 51, 0, 100 and then near 75, and the model follows each time. |
| SLIDER-04 | Given the Slider page on the web, when a visitor sets its bound slider to 70 and posts the form with the `Apply` button, then the answer has the slider at 70 and shows the command's status `Volume 70`. |
| SLIDER-05 | Given the `slider-range` fixture in each theme on the desktop (a 360 px `c:RangeSlider` from 0 to 1000 by 10 at 200 and 800 under a field title and description, and a vertical one from 0 to 100 at 20 and 60), and the `slider-states` fixture's range at 25 and 75, when they are shown, then each has two 16 px white thumbs with the 1 px `--primary` border and `shadow-sm` at shadcn/ui's boxes within 0.5 px, and its track is `--primary` between the thumbs and `--muted` before and after them, as Radix Slider's `Range` between its two `Thumb`s. |
| SLIDER-06 | Given a `c:RangeSlider` from 0 to 1000 by 10 whose `LowerValue` and `UpperValue` are bound two way at 200 and 800, on the desktop, when the pointer presses the track at 700 and drags to 750, presses at 100, when the lower thumb is dragged past the upper one, and when the keyboard presses Right, Shift+Right, PageDown, Home and End on a thumb and moves the lower thumb past the upper one, then the press moves the closest thumb (the upper to 700, then 750; the lower to 100), the values stay ordered (a thumb dragged or stepped past the other becomes the upper one and keeps the focus), Right steps by 10, Shift+Right and PageUp/PageDown by 100, Home sets the lower value to 0 and End the upper to 1000, every value snaps to 10, and the model follows each change, as Radix Slider does; a disabled one takes no input. |
| SLIDER-07 | Given the `slider-range` fixture in each theme on the web, when it is served and Chromium lays it out, then each `c:RangeSlider` is a group named by its `AutomationProperties.Name` holding two range inputs labelled `Minimum` and `Maximum` with their fields, minimum, maximum, step and values (Aioros's `a:Web.Range`), its two thumbs, read from the screenshot, are centered on shadcn/ui's within 1 px and 16 px across within 2 px (their antialiased borders), and its track is `--primary` between the thumbs and `--muted` before and after them, with no other color at its ends, as on the desktop (SLIDER-05). |
| SLIDER-08 | Given the Slider page's "Range binding" example in Chromium (a `c:RangeSlider` from 0 to 1000 by 10 bound to the model at 200 and 800), when the pointer presses the track at 700, the lower thumb is dragged past the upper one, the keys step the focused thumb (Right, Shift+Right, Left) and set the ends (Home, End), the pointer mapped over the track's whole width as Radix's, and the visitor presses `Apply budget`, then the thumbs move as SLIDER-06 states for the desktop and the answer shows the posted values in order in the command's status (`Budget $… - $…`). |
