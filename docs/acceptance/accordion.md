# Accordion

shadcn/ui's `accordion` (Radix Accordion) as `c:Accordion` (a sealed `ItemsControl` of Avalonia `Expander`s:
`IsMultiple`, `IsCollapsible`, `ContentTemplate`) with Cosmo's `Expander` theme: each item `border-b` (but the last), its
header `py-4 text-sm font-medium` with Lucide's `chevron-down` (16 px, `--muted-foreground`) turning 180° when open and
underlined under the pointer, its content `text-sm pb-4`, opening and closing its height over 200 ms (`HeightTransition`).

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| ACCORDION-01 | Given the `accordion-demo` fixture in each theme on the web, when the page is served, then each item is a `<details>` with its `<summary>` holding the header's text, the first `open` with its two paragraphs in shadcn/ui's 14 px font, line height and color, and the items separated by 1 px `--border` lines; the items of the single accordion share one `name` (HTML's exclusive `<details name>`, `a:Web.Exclusive`), so a click on the second summary opens it and closes the first, without a request (framework need 47) |
| ACCORDION-02 | Given the `accordion-demo` fixture in each theme on the desktop, when it is shown, then each header is 52 px high with its 14 px medium title and its 16 px chevron (turned 180° for the open item) at shadcn/ui's boxes within 1.5 px, the open item's paragraphs at shadcn/ui's boxes within 1.5 px with 16 px between them and 16 px below, every item but the last has a 1 px `--border` bottom line, and the header's text is underlined under the pointer. |
| ACCORDION-03 | Given single and multiple accordions on the desktop, when items are opened with the mouse and the keyboard (Space on a focused header), then a single accordion keeps one item open (opening another closes the first), refuses to close its open item unless `IsCollapsible`, and a multiple accordion keeps every opened item open. |
| ACCORDION-04 | Given an item opening on the desktop with Cosmo's `HeightTransition` on a clock the test moves, when the clock moves 0, 100 and 200 ms, then the content is 0 px, the eased share (`cubic-bezier(0, 0, 0.58, 1)` at half time, rounded up to the device pixel) and then its full height, the layout's height once done; closing runs the eased share back down, the content staying visible until the end and hidden once the 200 ms have passed. |
| ACCORDION-05 | Given a view whose model binds a `c:Accordion`'s `ItemsSource` (each item's header through `ItemTemplate`, its answer through `ContentTemplate`) and an item's `IsExpanded` two way (compiled bindings, no code-behind), when the model opens the third item and the user then opens the first, then the third item shows its model's answer and the model's open state follows the single accordion back to false. |
| ACCORDION-06 | Given the accordion page's Binding example on the web, whose first item binds `IsExpanded` two way to the model's `AccessibleOpen` and whose second item is not bound, when `Toggle the first answer` is pressed, then the answer opens the first item, and pressed again closes it, as the model says; when the visitor opens the first item by hand, then its state is posted as a change, and `Toggle the first answer` then closes it (the model had learned it was open); and the second item, opened by the visitor, stays open across these posts (audit INT-02). |
