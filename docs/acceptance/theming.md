# Theming

How an app applies the tokens: `CosmoTheme`, `ThemeScope` and resource overrides (`src/Aioros.Cosmo/Theming`), on the
desktop (Avalonia headless) and on the web (the Gallery).

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| THEME-01 | Given a window with `CosmoTheme` in the light variant, when it is shown, then its background is `CosmoBackgroundBrush` (`#FFFFFF`) and a `Button`'s background is `--primary` (`#171717`); when its `RequestedThemeVariant` becomes `Dark` at runtime, then the window background becomes `#0A0A0A` and the same button's background `#E5E5E5`, without recreating either. |
| THEME-02 | Given `<c:CosmoTheme Color="Blue" Radius="Large" />`, when a `Button` and a `c:Card` are shown, then the button's background is blue's `--primary` (`#1447E6`) and its corner radius `CosmoRadiusMd` for 0.75rem (9.6 px), and the card's corner radius `CosmoRadiusXl` (16.8 px); setting `Color` back to `Neutral` at runtime gives the button `#171717` again. |
| THEME-03 | Given an application whose resources override `CosmoPrimaryBrush` in their `Light` theme dictionary only (`#4F39F6`), when a `Button` is shown in each variant, then its background is the override in the light variant and Cosmo's `--primary` (`#E5E5E5`) in the dark one: an app's resources win over Cosmo's, per variant, as shadcn's CSS variables in `globals.css` do. |
| THEME-04 | Given a `ThemeScope` with `Color="Violet"` holding a `Button`, beside a `Button` outside it, when the scope's `Color` becomes `Green` and its `Radius` `None` at runtime, then the inner button's background becomes green's `--primary` and its corner radius 0, while the outer button keeps neutral's `#171717` and 8 px. |
| THEME-05 | Given the Gallery's theme customizer at `/themes?color=blue&radius=none`, when it is requested, then the preview's buttons are written with blue's `--primary` (`#1447e6`) and no corner radius while the page's own chrome keeps the neutral tokens, the color and radius links mark the current choice, and every choice answers from the page's plan (the page precompiles, GALLERY-01). |
| THEME-06 | Given the Gallery, when a visitor follows `/theme/dark` and then requests a page, then the page is rendered with the dark tokens (the body's background `#0a0a0a`, a default `Button` `#e5e5e5` on `#171717`), and after `/theme/light` with the light ones (`#ffffff`, `#171717` on `#fafafa`). |
| THEME-07 | Given the Popover page in Chromium asking for less motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`), when the visitor opens the popover and closes it with Escape, then no transition runs on it either way (its transition durations `0s`) and it opens and closes at once, at rest; the desktop has no platform setting to follow (Avalonia's `IPlatformSettings` exposes none), so an app sets `c:Motion.IsReduced` itself. |
| THEME-08 | Given a page with a button under an open popover and under an open dialog on the desktop, when each closes and the button is clicked while its exit plays, then the click reaches the button (a leaving overlay passes clicks through), and a popover opened again during its exit shows at once with no picture of it left. Precedent: Radix's `Presence`, whose leaving content no longer takes part in the page (`pointer-events` back on the body). |
| THEME-09 | Given a 14 px `TextBlock` marked `a:Web.Shimmer` in `#737373` on the desktop, in a window on a still clock, when it is shown and the clock moves 1 s and then 1 s more, then its foreground is a five-stop linear gradient at 110°, the text's color at its edges and at its middle a highlight of the text's color at a fifth of its alpha (in the dark theme its lightness raised by 0.4 to at least 0.8, its alpha plus 0.4), the highlight's center starting `3ch + 40px` before the text, halfway across after 1 s and back at the start after 2 s (from the far side right to left); a window asking for less motion (`c:Motion.IsReduced`) leaves the text its plain color. Precedent: the shadcn package 4.21.5's `shimmer` utility (`dist/tailwind.css`, its `--shimmer-color` from CSS Color 5's relative color syntax and its `shimmer` keyframes over 2 s, linear, endless), Aioros's TEXT-54 on the web. |
| THEME-10 | Given 100 px tall scroll viewers on the desktop, one marked `a:Web.ScrollFade` `y` and one `b` over 400 px of content and one `y` over 80 px, when they are laid out and scrolled, then at the top the first two are masked opaque from their top to 12 px above their bottom (a fade of `min(12%, 40px)`) and the third, whose content fits, is not masked; scrolled 48 px, the first fades its top over 6 px (half the fade, `ease-in-out` halfway through the first 96 px); and at the end the first fades only its top over 12 px and the second is not masked. Precedent: the shadcn package 4.21.5's `scroll-fade-y` and `scroll-fade-b` utilities (their fades revealed by scroll-driven animations over 96 px, `ease-in-out`), Aioros's LAYOUT-87 on the web. |
