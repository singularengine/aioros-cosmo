# Combobox

shadcn/ui's `combobox` (its Popover and Command composition, `combobox-demo`) as `c:Combobox` (a sealed
`SelectingItemsControl`: an outline trigger with `PlaceholderText` and Lucide's `chevrons-up-down`, a popover as wide as
the trigger holding a `c:Command` over its items with `SearchPlaceholder` and `Empty`; `SelectedItem`, `SelectedItems`
and `IsDropDownOpen` two way, `SelectionMode` `Single` toggling or `Multiple`, `ItemTemplate` or `DisplayMemberBinding`).

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| COMBOBOX-01 | Given the `combobox-demo` and `combobox-open` fixtures in each theme on the web, when the page is served, then the trigger is a 200 by 36 px button at shadcn/ui's box within 1 px with the outline look (1 px `--border` border, 8 px radius, medium 14 px `Select framework...`) and its chevrons, opening an anchored popover without a request; the popover holds the command's input as the WAI-ARIA `combobox` over a `listbox` of its five options (Next.js to Astro), and typing `as` shows only Astro, selected, without a request (`a:Web.Filter`, framework needs 57 and 58) |
| COMBOBOX-02 | Given the same fixtures in each theme on the desktop, when the trigger is clicked for `combobox-open`, then the trigger is at shadcn/ui's box within 1 px with its 12 px padding, medium text and a 16 px chevron at 50% opacity, and the popover is at shadcn/ui's box within 1.5 px (4 px under the trigger, as wide), with a 1 px `--border` border, an 8 px radius, `--popover` and `shadow-md`, its input row 36 px and its five items 32 px at shadcn/ui's boxes, the first selected in `--accent`. |
| COMBOBOX-03 | Given a combobox of five frameworks on the desktop, when the user opens it, types `re` and presses Enter, then Remix is chosen, the popover closes and the trigger reads `Remix`; reopened, the search is empty and only Remix shows its check; choosing Remix again clears the choice and the trigger reads its placeholder; Escape closes the popover without a change; and with `SelectionMode="Multiple"` choosing Next.js and Astro keeps the popover open, chooses both and shows them as two badges in the trigger, and choosing Astro again leaves Next.js alone. |
| COMBOBOX-04 | Given a view whose model gives framework objects (`ItemsSource`, `DisplayMemberBinding` with a compiled binding), binds `SelectedItem` and `IsDropDownOpen` two way and a multiple combobox's `SelectedItems` to an observable collection (no code-behind), when the user chooses SvelteKit, then the model's framework is SvelteKit; when the model sets Astro, the trigger reads `Astro`; when the model opens the drop-down, the popover shows; and choosing two frameworks in the multiple combobox fills the model's collection with both. |
| COMBOBOX-05 | Given the Combobox page on the web, its first demo unbound and its second bound, when the visitor chooses SvelteKit in the first, then with no request its trigger reads SvelteKit, SvelteKit's option alone is checked and the popover closes; choosing SvelteKit again shows the placeholder, still with no request; and when the visitor chooses SvelteKit again and then Nuxt.js in the second, then that choice alone posts, and the answer shows the first still SvelteKit, the second Nuxt.js and `Selected: Nuxt.js`: the combobox chooses in the browser as a native select does, its index a field of the form (`a:Web.Choice`, Aioros's need 75). |
