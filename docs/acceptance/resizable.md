# Resizable

shadcn/ui's `resizable` (react-resizable-panels 4.5.8) as `c:ResizablePanelGroup` (a sealed `Grid` laying its panels side
by side or stacked, each panel a star definition sized by its percentage), `c:ResizablePanel` (`DefaultSize`, `Size` two
way, `MinSize`, `MaxSize`, `IsCollapsible`, `CollapsedSize`, all percentages of the group) and `c:ResizableHandle`
(Avalonia's `GridSplitter`: a 1 px `--border` line, `WithHandle` for the 12 by 16 px grip with Lucide's `grip-vertical`;
the library's keys: the arrows by 5% along the group, Home and End to the limits, Enter to collapse, F6 to the next
handle).

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| RESIZABLE-01 | Given the `resizable-demo`, `resizable-handle` and `resizable-vertical` fixtures in each theme on the web, when the page is served, then each group is a CSS grid whose tracks keep the panels' percentages, the panels and the 1 px `--border` handles are at shadcn/ui's boxes within 1 px, and each handle is the WAI-ARIA window splitter: a focusable `role="separator"` (`tabindex="0"`) with its orientation, holding its template's part, its `aria-valuenow` the percentage of the panel before it (50 and 25 in the demo, 25 in the others) and its step shadcn's 5% (`a:Web.ResizeStep`), so ArrowRight (ArrowDown in the vertical group) moves the first handle to 30 (55 in the demo) without a request (framework need 55) |
