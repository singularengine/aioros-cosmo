# Sheet

shadcn/ui's `sheet` (Radix Dialog at an edge) as `c:Sheet` (`Side`: `Right`, `Left`, `Top`, `Bottom`): the dialog's
surface with the `sheet` class and its side (shadcn's `SheetContent`: a side sheet `w-3/4 sm:max-w-sm` as tall as its
host with a border on its inner edge, a top or bottom sheet as wide as its host), its header (`p-4 gap-1.5`, the title
16 px semibold) and footer (`mt-auto p-4`, a column of full-width buttons) styled inside it.

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| SHEET-01 | Given the `sheet-open` (right) and `sheet-top` fixtures on the desktop in each theme, when their triggers open them, then the right sheet is 384 px wide and as tall as the host at its right edge with a 1 px left border, no radius, shadcn's background and `shadow-lg`, its header 16 px from its edges with the title 16 px semibold on a 24 px line and the description 6 px below, its footer's buttons as wide as the sheet less 32 px at its bottom, 8 px apart; the top sheet is as wide as the host at its top with a 1 px bottom border; every box is the reference's within 0.5 px horizontally and 2.5 px vertically. |
| SHEET-02 | Given sheets on the left and at the bottom on the desktop, when they open, then the left sheet covers the host's left edge (three quarters of a host narrower than 512 px) with a 1 px right border, the bottom sheet the host's bottom edge with a 1 px top border; the focus starts on the first control and cycles inside, and Escape and a click on the mask close them with the focus back on the trigger. |
| SHEET-03 | Given a view whose model binds a sheet's `IsOpen` two way and its header's description to the model, when the model opens it on the desktop, then it opens at its `Side` showing the model's text, and closing it with Escape sets the model's value back to false. |
| SHEET-04 | Given the Sheet page on the web, when the trigger is pressed in Chromium, then the answer opens the page's `<dialog class="prompt">` (written closed with the page, `a:Web.Modal`) as a modal whose surface sits at the host's right edge (`justify-self:end`) as tall as the dialog, with its fields as named inputs; pressing its Close button posts and the answer closes it; Radix's slide animation is not written (framework need 37). |
| SHEET-05 | Given a sheet on each side on the desktop, when it opens, then its surface is shown its own width (right, left) or height (top, bottom) outside its side, with no fade or zoom (`slide-in-from-{side}`), its transform's transition over 500 ms with `ease-in-out` (`cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)`, shadcn/ui's `data-[state=open]:duration-500`), and its mask fades in over 150 ms; both are at rest once their time has passed, and in a window with `c:Motion.IsReduced` it opens at rest, nothing moving. |
