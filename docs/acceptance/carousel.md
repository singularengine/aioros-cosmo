# Carousel

shadcn/ui's `carousel` (Embla 8.5.2) as Avalonia's `Carousel` with Cosmo's implicit theme: the slides in a clipped
viewport (`ViewportFraction` for slides narrower than it, `PageSlide` over 300 ms), shadcn's previous and next buttons
(outline, 32 px, fully rounded, 48 px outside the viewport, turned for `vertical`) disabled at the ends unless
`WrapSelection` (Embla's `loop`), Left and Right while the focus is inside, swiping on touch screens and dragging with
the mouse (Embla's drag, on the web too), and Cosmo's
optional slide indicators (the `indicators` class, `c:CarouselIndicators`).

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| CAROUSEL-01 | Given the `carousel-demo`, `carousel-api` and `carousel-orientation` fixtures in each theme on the web, when the page is served, then each carousel is the WAI-ARIA carousel (`aria-roledescription="carousel"`): a scroll-snap strip along its axis (x, or y for `vertical`) of five slides showing the first one's card, with its previous and next buttons (`Previous slide` disabled at the start, `Next slide` enabled); the demo's cards are square (`c:AspectRatio` written as `aspect-ratio`), and the vertical strip is 196 px with slides of 146 px (framework needs 43 and 56) |
| CAROUSEL-02 | Given the same fixtures in each theme on the desktop, when they are shown, then the viewport, the first card and its content are at shadcn/ui's boxes within 1 px, the previous and next buttons are 32 px circles at shadcn/ui's boxes within 1 px (above and below the viewport and turned 90° for `vertical`) with an outline button's border, background and Lucide arrow, and the previous button is disabled (50% opacity) on the first slide. |
| CAROUSEL-03 | Given a carousel of five slides on the desktop, when its next button is clicked twice, then the third slide is selected; Left from a focused button selects the second and Right the third; at the last slide the next button is disabled and Right does nothing; with `WrapSelection` the next button stays enabled and Right selects the first slide; a click on the fourth indicator of a carousel with the `indicators` class selects the fourth slide and marks only its dot in `--primary`; and a slide changing runs a 300 ms `PageSlide` (vertical for `vertical`). |
| CAROUSEL-04 | Given a view whose model gives the slides (an observable collection with an item template) and binds the carousel's `SelectedIndex` two way, with a text bound to it through a converter showing `Slide 1 of 5` and a command adding a slide (compiled bindings, no code-behind), when the next button is clicked, then the model's index is 1 and the text reads `Slide 2 of 5`; when the model sets the index to 4, the fifth slide shows and the next button is disabled; and the add command makes the text read `Slide 5 of 6` with the next button enabled again. |
| CAROUSEL-05 | Given the carousel page's Binding example on the web, whose `SelectedIndex` is bound two way to the model's `SlideIndex` with the strip scrolling smoothly, when its next button is clicked once, then exactly one post names the second slide and the model's text reads `Slide 2 of 5`; when it is clicked twice quickly, then the strip and the model end on the fourth slide (`Slide 4 of 5`) and the last post names it (audit INT-06). |
| CAROUSEL-06 | Given the Carousel page's first example in Chromium on a controlled clock, when the mouse drags its strip 80 px left, holds it 200 ms and lets go, then the strip settles back on the first slide; when it drags it 60 px left and lets go at once (a flick), the strip goes to the second slide, as Embla's drag does with a mouse; the strip is marked `data-aioros-drag` from Cosmo's theme (`IsSwipeEnabled`) (framework need 103). |
| CAROUSEL-07 | Given the Carousel page's Binding example on the web (slides from the model with an item template, `SelectedIndex` two way, the `indicators` class, the model's `Slide N of M` text and its Add a slide command), when the fourth indicator is clicked, then one request posts the index, the text reads `Slide 4 of 5` and only the fourth dot is in `--primary`; when Add a slide is clicked, then the strip has six slides and six dots and the text reads `Slide 4 of 6`. |
