# Avatar

shadcn/ui's `avatar` (Radix Avatar) as `c:Avatar` (a sealed `ContentControl` with `Source`, its content the fallback) and
`c:AvatarGroup` (a sealed `ItemsControl` of avatars or of an `ItemTemplate`'s avatars): a 32 px circle (`sm` 24, `lg` 40) showing its image, or its fallback initials on
`--muted` while it has none; in a group, avatars overlap by 8 px, each ringed 2 px with `--background`.

| AC | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| AVATAR-01 | Given the `avatar-demo` fixture in each theme on the web, when the page is served, then each avatar's image is an `<img>` with its PNG and Chromium computes every avatar at shadcn/ui's position and size within 1 px (24, 32 and 40 px), the round ones fully rounded and the `rounded-lg` one 10 px, the fallbacks with `--muted` backgrounds and `--muted-foreground` 14 px (12 px for `sm`) text, and the grouped avatars 24 px apart with the 2 px `--background` ring as a box shadow, each `<img>`'s `alt` its avatar's automation name, each image clipped by its rounded part (`overflow:hidden` with the radius; framework need 45, resolved in Aioros `0.1.0-preview.408.cc55357`). |
| AVATAR-02 | Given the `avatar-demo` fixture in each theme on the desktop, when it is shown, then each `c:Avatar` has shadcn/ui's box within 1 px, clips its image to its corner radius, shows its content, the fallback (centered, `--muted` and `--muted-foreground`), only while `Source` is null, and each grouped avatar overlaps the previous by 8 px (every item 8 px to the left, the group's items 8 px in, so a templated list is written once for every item) with the `--background` ring (`CosmoRingBackgroundShadow`). |
| AVATAR-03 | Given a view whose model binds `c:Avatar`'s `Source` and content (the initials computed from a bound name through a converter) and a `c:AvatarGroup` of members whose `ItemTemplate` is an avatar (compiled bindings, no code-behind), when the model clears a member's picture and adds a member through a command, then that avatar shows the fallback initials and the group shows one more avatar overlapping the previous one. |
| AVATAR-04 | Given the Avatar page on the web, when a visitor posts `Add member` (a command adding a member to the bound collection), then the answer's bound group (an `ItemTemplate` whose avatar's content is a text bound to the member's initials) holds one more avatar reading `RP` and the text `4 members`; a bound `Source` or `Content` of `c:Avatar` is not planned on the web (framework need 21). |
| AVATAR-05 | Given the `avatar-badge` and `avatar-group-count` fixtures in each theme on the desktop, when they are laid out, then each `c:AvatarBadge` (an avatar's `Badge`) is shadcn/ui's 8, 10 or 12 px `--primary` dot at the bottom right of its `sm`, default or `lg` avatar, inside the avatar's circle, ringed with `--background`, its icon hidden in a `sm` avatar; and each `c:AvatarGroupCount` closing an avatar group overlaps its last avatar by 8 px as a 24, 32 or 40 px `--muted` circle (the size of the group's avatars, as shadcn/ui's `group-has-data-[size]`) with its text in 14 px `--muted-foreground`; every box within 1.5 px of shadcn/ui's. |
| AVATAR-06 | Given the `avatar-badge` and `avatar-group-count` fixtures in each theme on the web, when Chromium lays them out, then every avatar, badge and group count stands at shadcn/ui's box within 1 px with its background and text colors, as AVATAR-05 states for the desktop. |
