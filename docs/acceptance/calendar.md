# Calendar

shadcn/ui's `calendar` (react-day-picker 9.8.1) as Avalonia's `Calendar` with Cosmo's themes: a 12 px padded box whose
32 px header holds ghost previous and next buttons around the month's name, the weekdays' short names in the muted
foreground, and 32 px day buttons a week apart by 8 px, only the weeks that hold a day of the month (`c:CalendarDays`).
The selected day is `--primary`, a range's ends `--primary` joined by an `--accent` band rounded at the ends of each
week, the other months' days muted (or hidden with `outside-hidden`), blackout days at 50% (`disabled`). The selection
binds two way: `SelectedDate` for one day, `c:CalendarSelection.RangeStart` and `RangeEnd` for a range, and
`c:CalendarSelection.Dates` for several days.
