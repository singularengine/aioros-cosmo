# Navigation Menu

shadcn/ui's `navigation-menu` (Radix NavigationMenu with `viewport={false}`) as `c:NavigationMenu` (a sealed
`StackPanel`: its items 4 px apart, Radix's hover and switching delays through a `TimeProvider`, Left and Right between
items) with `c:NavigationMenuTrigger`s (sealed `Button`s whose `Flyout` is the content: 36 px, medium, `px-4`, Lucide's
12 px chevron turning while open, the content 6 px under the trigger's start in the `navigation-menu` look), links as
buttons or hyperlink buttons with the `navigation-menu-link` class and plain links with `navigation-menu-trigger`.
